Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

YouTube AI is a creator tool that helps users create, render, publish, and review the performance of their own video projects.

Information we process

When you sign in or connect YouTube, the service may process account identifiers, basic channel information, authorization tokens, project content, rendered videos, publishing settings, and YouTube analytics needed to provide the features you request.

Google and YouTube data

Google/YouTube authorization is used only to provide connected features such as reading channel information and trends, publishing videos when you explicitly request it, and retrieving analytics. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in encrypted form on the server and are not intentionally exposed to the browser or public source code.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, operate the creator workflow, render and store your projects, connect requested YouTube features, troubleshoot the service, and improve functionality. We do not claim ownership of your original content.

Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app, such as hosting, database/storage, GitHub Actions, Google, and YouTube. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Data may be retained while needed to operate the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

Your choices

You can choose whether to connect YouTube and whether to publish a video. You can revoke the app's Google access through your Google Account permissions. Requests concerning stored account data can be sent using the contact information associated with the service.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of YouTube AI through the contact information provided with the service.

This policy is general information and is not legal advice. Applicable privacy obligations can vary by location and how the service is offered.
