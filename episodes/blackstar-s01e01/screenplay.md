# BLACKSTAR — S1E1: FIRST CONTACT

**Target runtime:** ~20 minutes  
**Format:** Original cinematic tactical military science-fiction animation  
**Rating:** Teen  
**Continuity rule:** Faces, uniforms, weapons, damage, dirt, ability colors, room tone, and tactical positions carry forward unless changed on screen.

## 1. COLD OPEN — ORBIT ABOVE EREBUS

Black screen. Broken emergency fragments hiss through static. A distress tone begins, cuts off before completing, and never repeats.

A field of orbital debris drifts over Erebus. Small fragments rotate past camera. The colony below is intact, lights still burning.

Inside the descending dropship, KESTREL appears as a cyan human face reflected across the cockpit glass.

**KESTREL:** Erebus Colony stopped transmitting eleven hours ago.

**VOSS:** No distress beacon?

**KESTREL:** None.

Voss studies the silent settlement below.

**VOSS:** Then somebody wanted silence.

The dropship tips into atmosphere.

## 2. ROLES

The squad rides helmet-off so their faces are clearly established. Voss draws sectors across a holo-map. Jax locks the MANTA-H into his harness. Vale checks the white diagnostic lines on her Null Loom bracer. Rook scatters Ghostline sensor pucks across the deck and catches them as the ship banks.

**VOSS:** Roles stay clean. Rook, eyes. Vale, kill their toys. Jax, open what will not open.

**JAX:** Finally, a mission briefing I understand.

A Ghostline puck turns green in Rook's palm.

**ROOK:** Ghostline live.

Kestrel overlays the landing approach.

**KESTREL:** Surface defenses are awake. They are simply not aiming at us.

Voss looks up.

**VOSS:** Yet.

## 3. THE EMPTY COLONY

The ramp opens into dust. BLACKSTAR exits in formation. Visors remain open long enough to preserve face recognition.

Rook's Ghostline overlay paints faint movement probabilities beneath the streets, but nothing on the surface.

**ROOK:** No bodies. No evacuation traffic. Ghostline has nothing above ground.

Voss points two fingers toward the main street.

**VOSS:** Then we follow what it does have.

The squad moves through a plaza. A child's toy rotates slowly under a ventilation fan. No one comments on it.

## 4. SOMETHING BELOW

Untouched meals sit in a cafeteria. Apartment doors stand open. Industrial machinery is still running as though the workers stepped away seconds ago.

Vale checks the power grid.

**VALE:** Grid is carrying full load.

Rook kneels, rotates a sensor puck, and watches a faint line crawl under the pavement.

**ROOK:** Movement below us. Slow. Organized.

**VOSS:** Mark the route. Nobody outruns Rook's picture.

The line stops directly beneath them.

A beat.

Then it continues away.

## 5. BREACH

A service hatch is welded shut by material that does not belong on a human colony.

Jax braces the MANTA-H.

**JAX:** Knock.

He fires a fictional breach pulse. The alien-welded seam fractures and the hatch folds inward.

**VALE:** That was structurally unnecessary.

**JAX:** Yet emotionally correct.

Voss places an Aegis marker behind the team. A cyan field outlines the rear approach.

**VOSS:** Aegis on the rear lane. If it lights red, nothing crosses it.

They enter the elevator.

## 6. ALIEN CUTS

The elevator descends through fog and intermittent industrial light. Faces appear and disappear in bands of amber illumination.

Fresh geometric cuts scar the underground walls.

Vale scans residue.

**VALE:** This cut is hours old. Not human tooling.

Jax lifts the MANTA-H.

**JAX:** Still want me opening it?

Vale raises one finger without looking at him.

**VALE:** I want you not touching it for five seconds.

Her Null Loom locks onto an unknown device buried in the wall.

## 7. FIRST DEVICE

An alien cable has been fused directly into the colony grid. Vale projects a counter-pattern. A dormant drone hanging from the ceiling twitches, then freezes.

**VALE:** One device denied.

**VOSS:** One?

Rook's eyes snap over Vale's shoulder.

**ROOK:** Two. Behind her.

A second drone eye opens.

**VALE:** Correcting.

## 8. FIRST CONTACT

Rook calls the threat before it fires.

**ROOK:** Contact rear-left!

Voss pulls Vale off the line. Jax drives forward.

**JAX:** Mine.

The MANTA-H knocks the drone into a support beam. Vale reaches past Jax and kills its uplink with the Null Loom.

**VALE:** Uplink is mine.

A pulse of light races halfway down the corridor and dies.

**KESTREL:** Their network noticed both of you.

Silence.

Then every light in the facility turns on at once.

## 9. THE WRONG DOOR

Activation waves race through the tunnels. Voss's Aegis lane flashes cyan, then red.

A Veyr soldier steps into it, recoils, and withdraws without panic.

**VOSS:** They are probing the grid. Good. Let them learn the wrong door.

Rook watches three probabilistic tracks form on his display.

**ROOK:** New route, right side.

**JAX:** See? Recon earns his food.

The squad shifts corridors before the Veyr commit.

## 10. THE VEYR

A Veyr squad emerges through smoke: segmented ceramic armor, luminous masks, deliberate movement.

Rook's tracks split.

Vale sweeps her bracer and a drone cluster falls dark.

**VOSS:** Controlled withdrawal. Rook, find the flank. Vale, blind their support.

Jax looks back at her while firing controlled fictional energy bursts.

**JAX:** And me?

**VOSS:** Be obvious.

Jax grins.

**JAX:** My specialty.

## 11. STOP BEING STEERABLE

Jax anchors the center. Rook climbs an upper service ledge and moves parallel to the fight. Vale disables an industrial safety lock, then swings a dormant manipulator arm into the Veyr approach.

Voss places Aegis across a side entrance.

**VALE:** Their shots are steering us.

**VOSS:** Then stop being steerable. Rook?

Rook looks down from above.

**ROOK:** Side path is clear for twelve seconds.

**JAX:** Luxury.

The team crosses. A blast door seals behind them.

## 12. ZERO-G ROUTE

Gravity fails. Debris rises slowly from the floor.

**ROOK:** Gravity is gone.

**KESTREL:** Redirected, not gone. I have a route.

A safe-vector overlay appears along an exterior maintenance shaft.

**VOSS:** Put it on Rook. He leads.

Rook pushes off first. The others follow across a collapsing gantry while Kestrel updates vectors in real time.

## 13. HOLD THE HOLE

At reactor level, a heavy Veyr push blocks the direct route.

Jax plants the MANTA-H and fires a breach pulse into a reinforced maintenance bypass.

**JAX:** Door is mine.

**VOSS:** Open it and hold the hole.

Jax checks the opening, then checks Voss.

**JAX:** Two jobs. Premium rate.

Voss marks an Aegis lane while the team advances through the bypass.

## 14. THE GATEWAY

Jax breaks the final lock.

The chamber beyond is enormous. A ring-like machine fills the space without resembling any human transport system.

Vale scans residue along the platform.

**VALE:** They did not kill the colonists. They moved them.

Rook looks up at vertical mechanical spines disappearing into darkness.

**ROOK:** Where?

Vale watches impossible coordinates resolve and collapse.

**VALE:** Somewhere this machine can reach.

**KESTREL:** And it is preparing to do it again.

## 15. IT KNOWS HER NAME

Portal geometry begins to form. Voss records everything.

A taller Veyr figure steps through: the Commander.

It pauses over old Aegis markers, then looks directly at Voss.

**VOSS:** This is reconnaissance.

**KESTREL:** Energy growth is exponential.

The Commander speaks through a broken translation layer.

**VEYR COMMANDER:** Mara Voss.

Voss's expression changes for the first time.

**VOSS:** Nobody told you my name.

## 16. SAME ROLES

Voss drops a lane denial. The Commander changes direction before it fully forms.

She realizes it has learned the tactic.

**VOSS:** Ninety seconds. Same roles. Jax, hold. Vale, deny. Rook, climb. Kestrel, extraction.

Jax takes the corridor. Vale runs for the coupling bank. Rook starts up the vertical spine.

**JAX:** And you?

Voss squares herself toward the Commander.

**VOSS:** I keep it looking at me.

## 17. CHANGE PATTERN

Voss fights the Commander with a cracked visor and visible face. Jax breach-pulses cover into a new firing gap. Vale forces the first gateway coupling out of sync. Rook climbs by following Ghostline returns through moving machinery.

**VALE:** First coupling down.

**ROOK:** Twelve meters.

**JAX:** You have six.

The Commander anticipates Voss's familiar sidestep and nearly catches her.

**VOSS:** Change pattern. It learns repetition.

She deliberately breaks her own rhythm.

## 18. THE SHORTEST ROUTE

Rook reaches the upper node and plants a fictional disruptor. Vale disables the second safety system.

**ROOK:** Set!

**VALE:** Run.

Voss's Aegis field fails as the Commander adapts around it.

Outside, Kestrel spoofs the facility's tracking network.

**KESTREL:** I have selected the shortest route.

A dropship warning flashes on Voss's visor.

**VOSS:** That is a wall.

The dropship smashes through a non-load-bearing exterior section.

**KESTREL:** It was.

## 19. EXTRACTION

The ramp opens while the dropship is still moving.

Jax boards backward, covering the others. Vale drags Rook across the ramp after a hard landing. Voss stays last.

**JAX:** Commander!

**VOSS:** Go!

**KESTREL:** Already doing so.

Vale watches the network map collapse.

**VALE:** Their network is collapsing.

Rook looks at one surviving line.

**ROOK:** Not all of it.

Voss jumps aboard. The gateway chamber folds into darkness behind them.

## 20. THE SCALE OF IT

Quiet orbit. No music at first.

The squad sits helmet-off, exhausted. Dirt and damage remain exactly where the fight put them.

Erebus turns below the viewport.

**ROOK:** We stopped it.

Kestrel's face appears in the glass. One signal blinks on the dark hemisphere. Then ten. Then hundreds.

**KESTREL:** Commander... that was not the only signal.

Voss stands.

**VOSS:** How many?

More signals appear beyond the horizon.

**KESTREL:** I am still counting.

CUT TO another location. A rust-red RED VECTOR insignia appears on an encrypted receiver. A hand copies BLACKSTAR's gateway packet.

**UNKNOWN RED VECTOR VOICE:** Copy the packet. BLACKSTAR found the door.

Cut to black.

**TITLE:** BLACKSTAR

**END OF EPISODE 1**
